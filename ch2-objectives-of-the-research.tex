\chapter{Objectives of the Research}

The objective if to provide a secure, modular and portable architecture for a TOTP authenticator on a USB stick, and a software solution implementing it.

\app{} replaces a regular authenticator app like Microsoft's Authenticator or Google Authenticator. For these, you need a mobile phone. If you don't have a dedicated work phone, you might need to tie your personal device to the TOTP verification of a work account. In case you lose your phone, your company might require to wipe your data. Furthermore, mixing personal belongings with work is not a good practice. A USB device is cheap, so it is not an overhead to have a USB device solely for authentication purposes.

While professional solutions like YubiKeys exist, they are quite expesive due to their dedicated hardware and branding. While they are far more secure than a simple USB stick, for two-factor authentication \app{} is more than enough.
